# Microsoft Project vs Planner Premium

Choosing the correct project management platform depends on the complexity of your projects and your organization's collaboration requirements.

## Microsoft Project

Suitable for:

- Traditional project management
- Complex scheduling
- Resource planning

## Planner Premium

Suitable for:

- Collaborative work management
- Microsoft Teams integration
- Modern cloud-first organizations

## Conclusion

Microsoft's long-term strategy increasingly centers around Planner Premium as the unified work management experience.
