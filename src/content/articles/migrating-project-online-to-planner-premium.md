# Migrating from Project Online to Planner Premium

Migration is more than moving projects from one platform to another. Organizations should understand existing workloads, licensing, integrations, and business processes before beginning the transition.

## Assess your environment

- Review active projects
- Identify custom workflows
- Audit integrations

## Prepare users

- Train project managers
- Introduce Planner Premium
- Run pilot migrations

## Best Practices

> Always validate your migration process in a non-production environment before moving business-critical projects.

## Conclusion

A phased migration strategy minimizes risk while helping users adapt to Microsoft's modern work management platform.
